Privacy Policy

How account, order, payment, cookie, support, email, Discord, Google login, and security information may be collected and used.

Information we collect

We may collect information you provide, such as name, email address, account details, Discord or Google login identifiers, support messages, application submissions, order details, product access records, download records, license/key delivery records, and admin communication preferences.

Payment information

Payments are processed by third-party payment providers. We may receive limited payment status details, order identifiers, fraud/risk signals, billing contact details, and transaction metadata, but we do not need to store full card numbers.

Cookies and device data

We may collect cookies, session identifiers, IP address, browser/device details, timestamps, referrers, pages visited, checkout events, and security logs to keep accounts logged in, protect the site, process purchases, improve performance, and prevent abuse.

How information is used

Information may be used to create and secure accounts, send OTP or reset codes, process purchases, deliver products and keys, manage downloads, provide support, send policy or service updates, prevent fraud, enforce terms, improve the site, and comply with legal or payment requirements.

Emails and notifications

We may send transactional emails or messages related to verification, password resets, orders, product keys, downloads, support, refunds, security, policy updates, and important service notices. Marketing-style messages may include an unsubscribe or preference method when required.

Sharing and processors

We may share necessary information with service providers such as payment processors, hosting providers, email providers, database/storage providers, analytics/security tools, Discord/Google integrations, and support tools. These providers process data according to their own terms and privacy practices.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, but no website, database, payment flow, email system, Discord integration, or hosting provider can be guaranteed 100% secure. You should use a strong password and keep your email/Discord account secure.

Retention

We may keep account, order, support, fraud-prevention, tax, accounting, legal, and security records as long as needed for operations, compliance, dispute handling, backups, and enforcement. Deleted accounts may still leave limited retained records where necessary.

Your choices

You can contact support to request help with account information, privacy questions, data correction, or account deletion. Some requests may be limited where records are needed for orders, security, fraud prevention, legal reasons, or payment disputes.

Policy updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated when features, integrations, payment methods, account systems, cookies, or legal requirements change. The Last updated date shows the current version.

These policies are designed to be clear and protective for digital products, accounts, downloads, keys, payments, support, and site access. Nothing here limits rights that cannot legally be limited.
