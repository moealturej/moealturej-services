What cookies are

Cookies and similar storage technologies are small browser records used to keep the site working, remember preferences, secure sessions, and understand basic performance.

Essential cookies

Essential cookies may be used for login sessions, CSRF protection, cart behavior, checkout flow, account security, rate limiting, fraud prevention, and remembering whether certain notices were dismissed. These are needed for core site functions.

Preference and performance cookies

We may use cookies or local storage for theme behavior, cart data, UI preferences, analytics, performance measurement, and error/security logging.

Third-party cookies

Payment providers, Discord, Google, email/security tools, analytics providers, hosting/CDN services, and embedded checkout tools may set or read their own cookies when their features are used. Their policies control their cookies.

Managing cookies

You can block or delete cookies through your browser. Some features, including login, cart, checkout, security checks, account pages, and product delivery, may not work correctly without required cookies.

Updates

This Cookie Policy may be updated as the site, checkout methods, analytics, account systems, or security tools change. The Last updated date shows the current version.

These policies are designed to be clear and protective for digital products, accounts, downloads, keys, payments, support, and site access. Nothing here limits rights that cannot legally be limited.
