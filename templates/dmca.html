Copyright respect

If you believe content on moealturej Services infringes your copyright or intellectual property rights, contact support with enough information for us to review the claim.

Required notice details

Include your name, contact email, the copyrighted work you claim is being infringed, the exact URL or product/page involved, a statement that you believe the use is unauthorized, and a statement that your notice is accurate.

Review process

We may remove, restrict, or disable access to content while reviewing a claim. We may request more information, reject incomplete claims, or restore content if a claim appears invalid.

False claims

Submitting false, misleading, or abusive takedown claims may have legal consequences and may result in blocked contact or account restrictions.

These policies are designed to be clear and protective for digital products, accounts, downloads, keys, payments, support, and site access. Nothing here limits rights that cannot legally be limited.
